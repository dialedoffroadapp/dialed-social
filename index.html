Dialed Offroad

Dirt bike suspension setup, built by a rider in Boise, Idaho.

Dialed gives you a starting point for your clickers, sag and tire pressure from your bike, your weight and where you ride, then helps you refine it one change at a time from how the bike felt. The numbers are a starting point you refine on the bike, never a law.
